Beautiful Beaches of Goa

Calangute Beach

Known as the "Queen of Beaches," this is Goa's largest and most popular beach with golden sands and vibrant atmosphere.

Palolem Beach

A serene crescent-shaped beach in South Goa, perfect for swimming and dolphin watching.

Vagator Beach

Famous for its red cliffs, rocky outcrops, and stunning sunset views over the Arabian Sea.
